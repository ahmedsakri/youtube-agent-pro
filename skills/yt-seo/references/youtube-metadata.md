# YouTube metadata: verified rules and practical implications

Checked against official YouTube Help on **9 October 2026**. Recheck these sources when a platform
limit or feature availability is central to the user's task. Recommendations below do not establish
that a change will improve a particular video's performance.

| Topic | Verified guidance | Use in this skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Titles and descriptions | Titles allow 100 characters; descriptions allow 5,000. [Edit video settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404?hl=en) | Count final fields, including added links/hashtags. Do not treat 40/60-character preview checks as upload limits. |
| Description relevance | YouTube recommends video-specific descriptions and one or two principal topic words in the title and description. [Description tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en) | Express the main topic naturally; distinct videos need distinct summaries. The full allowance is not a writing target. |
| Tags | Tags can help with common misspellings and otherwise play a minimal role; excessive tags in descriptions violate spam guidance. [Add tags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en) | Use limited, relevant disambiguation tags in their field. Do not present tag volume as a growth technique. |
| Hashtags | They link related content and search results. Unrelated hashtags are disallowed; beyond 60, all hashtags on the content are ignored. [Hashtag guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en) | Optional, relevant hashtags are valid. Omit them when requested; do not say they are useless. |
| Shorts and Search | Shorts recommendations consider viewer choice, viewing behavior, and personalization. Search also considers metadata match and whether viewers click and watch. [Shorts discovery](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en) | Keep search metadata and feed-performance diagnosis separate. No keyword list guarantees distribution. |
| Links | URLs in Shorts descriptions/comments are non-clickable; channel profile and related-video links are clickable. External clickable links in long-form descriptions require advanced features. [Sharing links](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en) | Use an accurate, destination-specific CTA only after verifying the destination exists. |
| Localization | YouTube accepts translated titles/descriptions for viewers in other languages. [Translate metadata](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6289575?hl=en) | Draft each requested locale separately and preserve the video's facts and intended audience. |

The numerical text scores bundled with the pack are writing heuristics, not YouTube ranking scores,
keyword-volume data, or evidence that a draft will outperform another. For actual performance,
compare the same format, traffic source, publication age, and the channel's own baseline.
